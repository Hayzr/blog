You've found my blog. Congratulations, and I'm sorry.

This is where I put things that don't fit in a conversation, a group chat, or a therapist's appointment — thoughts, opinions, half-baked theories, and the occasional rant about something nobody asked about.

There's no niche here. No content strategy. No target demographic. Just me, a keyboard, and an apparently inexhaustible supply of things to say about things.

If you read something you disagree with, feel free to stew on it. There are no comments. I've chosen to interpret this as a feature rather than a limitation.

Stick around if you like. Or don't. I'm a blog, not a bouncer.
